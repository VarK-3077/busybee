Page 12 · Management discussion and analysisOpen in PDF ↗

c2

The financial year was marked by a recovery in European demand for technical fabrics after two subdued years, partly offset by softer domestic apparel offtake in the second half.

c3

Revenue from operations rose to ₹1,284.6 crore in FY2025 from ₹1,102.3 crore in FY2024, an increase of 16.5 per cent, driven by higher export volumes of technical fabrics to European customers. Realisations were broadly stable.

c4

Export revenue accounted for 58 per cent of total revenue (FY2024: 51 per cent). The top five customers together contributed 41 per cent of revenue, and no single customer exceeded 12 per cent.

c1

Note 18 — Borrowings. Total borrowings stood at ₹462.0 crore as at 31 March 2025, of which ₹318.5 crore is long-term term loans and ₹143.5 crore working-capital facilities. Cash and bank balances were ₹49.4 crore.

c2

Term loans are secured by a first pari-passu charge on the property, plant and equipment of the Company and carry interest at rates linked to the lenders' one-year MCLR.
