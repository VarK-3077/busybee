def leverage_within_ceiling (net_debt ebitda : Int) : Prop :=
  0 < ebitda ∧ 2 * net_debt ≤ 7 * ebitda

EBITDA is positive, and two times net debt is at most seven times EBITDA — that is, net debt is at most 3.5 times EBITDA.
