# Acme Textiles Q1 profit rises; revenue momentum continues

*Synthetic news item. Source: fictional "Textile Trade Weekly". Date: 5 August 2025.*

Acme Textiles Limited reported revenue of Rs 238.4 crore for the quarter ended June 2025, up 9% from a year earlier, while net profit rose to Rs 10.2 crore from Rs 9.1 crore. Operating margin was 13.6%.

The report noted that the company had closed FY25 with revenue of Rs 1,018 crore, and that its borrowings stood at Rs 382 crore at the end of March. The company's cotton costs rose about 6% in the quarter, which it partly passed on to export customers.

Management said working-capital days remained elevated and that inventory would be reduced by the second half. The company is in talks with lenders for the term loan to fund its spinning expansion.
