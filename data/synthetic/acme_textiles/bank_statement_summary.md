# Bank Statement Summary — Acme Textiles Limited

*Synthetic summary of operating-account conduct prepared from statements for April 2024 to March 2025 (12 months). Primary account: Lakshmi Commercial Bank, cash credit account, fictional.*

## Account conduct

* Sanctioned fund-based working-capital limits: Rs 190.0 crore (cash credit Rs 140.0 crore and packing credit Rs 50.0 crore).
* Average monthly utilisation of fund-based limits: 84%. Peak utilisation was 97% in October 2024; month-end utilisation at 31 March 2025 was Rs 168.0 crore (88.4%).
* Average monthly credits to the operating account: Rs 79.4 crore, annualised Rs 952.8 crore, broadly consistent with reported revenue plus GST.
* Overdrawn beyond the sanctioned limit for more than 5 consecutive days: 2 instances (October 2024 for 7 days and November 2024 for 6 days), regularised from export proceeds.
* Cheque or ECS returns for insufficient funds: none. Delay in interest or instalment servicing: none.
* Letters of credit opened: Rs 41.6 crore during the year, all honoured at maturity; no devolvement.

## Concentration of credits

The five largest remitters accounted for 38% of credits. Export proceeds were 33% of credits. No unusual round-tripping or related-party credits were identified.
