# Acme Textiles to add 60,000 spindles in Rs 120 crore expansion

*Synthetic news item. Source: fictional "Southern Business Courier". Date: 18 July 2025.*

Tiruppur-based Acme Textiles Limited said on Friday that its board has cleared a Rs 120 crore capital expenditure programme to add 60,000 spindles at a new spinning unit in Tamil Nadu, with commercial production expected by the third quarter of FY27.

The company will fund the project through a Rs 78 crore term loan and Rs 42 crore of internal accruals. A senior executive said the company expects net debt to EBITDA to stay below 3.5 times during the construction period and that lenders have already consented to the project.

Acme exported 34% of its output in FY25, with the United Kingdom and Germany its largest markets. Analysts said the expansion would raise yarn capacity by about 17% and reduce dependence on purchased yarn for its garment business.

Meridian Ratings had on 12 June reaffirmed a long-term rating of MR A+ with a Stable outlook on the company's bank facilities.
