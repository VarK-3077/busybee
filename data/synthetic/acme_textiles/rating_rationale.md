# Meridian Ratings: Rating Rationale — Acme Textiles Limited

Date: 12 June 2025

## Rating action

Total bank facilities rated: Rs 432.0 crore (long-term term loans Rs 214.0 crore; fund-based working capital Rs 190.0 crore; non-fund-based limits Rs 28.0 crore)

Long-term rating: MR A+ / Stable (reaffirmed)
Short-term rating: MR A1 (reaffirmed)

Meridian Ratings has reaffirmed its ratings on the bank facilities of Acme Textiles Limited (Acme). The outlook is Stable.

## Rationale

The ratings reflect Acme's established position in cotton yarn and knitted garments, its integrated operations, growing export share and comfortable net worth. These strengths are partially offset by a working-capital-intensive business, exposure to cotton price volatility, moderate customer concentration and a debt-funded capital expenditure programme that will lift leverage over the medium term.

### Key rating strengths

* Integrated operations and established customer relationships. Exports were 34% of FY25 revenue and the top five customers contributed 41%.
* Healthy growth: revenue grew 10.1% to Rs 931.2 crore in FY25 and operating margin remained steady at 14.0%.
* Comfortable capital structure: net worth of Rs 300.0 crore and gearing (total debt to net worth) of 1.27 times at March 2025.

### Key rating weaknesses

* Working-capital-intensive operations: gross current assets were about 160 days of revenue, and average utilisation of fund-based limits was 84% over the 12 months to March 2025.
* Exposure to cotton price volatility and a thin pass-through ability in the domestic market.
* Debt-funded capex: the Rs 120 crore expansion is to be funded by a Rs 78 crore term loan, which will raise net debt to EBITDA to about 3.0 times in FY27 from 2.7 times in FY25.

## Financial risk profile

Interest coverage was 3.4 times in FY25 (FY24: 3.5 times). The debt service coverage ratio was 1.78 times. Net cash accruals were Rs 84.5 crore against annual term debt repayments of Rs 28.0 crore. The current ratio was 1.27 times. Free cash flow was thin at Rs 8.4 crore after capex of Rs 62.0 crore.

Liquidity is adequate. Bank limit utilisation averaged 84%. The company has no instances of delayed debt servicing. Unencumbered cash and liquid investments were Rs 30.0 crore at year end.

## Governance

Promoters held 58.4% of equity at March 2025 and 12.0% of their holding was pledged. The statutory auditors' opinion is unmodified; the auditors have drawn attention to an unprovided GST dispute of Rs 14.2 crore. Contingent liabilities were 7.2% of net worth.

## Rating sensitivity factors

Upward factors: sustained interest coverage above 4.0 times, net debt to EBITDA below 2.0 times and improvement in working-capital cycle.

Downward factors: net debt to EBITDA above 3.5 times, interest coverage below 2.5 times, a stretch in the working-capital cycle by more than 20 days, or a rise in promoter pledge above 25% of their holding.
