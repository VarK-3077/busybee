# Financial Covenants — Acme Textiles Limited

*Synthetic covenant sheet summarising the sanction terms agreed with the consortium lenders (lead bank: Lakshmi Commercial Bank, fictional). Sanction date: 28 September 2023; reviewed 30 April 2025. Amounts in Rs crore.*

## Facilities

* Fund-based working capital: Rs 190.0 crore (cash credit Rs 140.0 crore and packing credit Rs 50.0 crore).
* Term loans outstanding: Rs 214.0 crore at 31 March 2025.
* Non-fund-based limits (letters of credit and guarantees): Rs 28.0 crore.

## Other covenants

* Promoter pledge must not exceed 25% of promoter holding (actual 12.0%).
* Promoters' shareholding must remain at or above 51% (actual 58.4%).
* No dividend may be paid if any financial covenant is breached. The dividend of Rs 1.00 per share (Rs 2.4 crore) for FY25 is within the permitted amount.
* The borrower must submit monthly stock and book-debt statements within 21 days of month end and audited accounts within 6 months of year end.
* Prior consent of lenders is required for capital expenditure exceeding Rs 50 crore in a year. Consent for the Rs 120 crore spinning expansion was granted on 30 April 2025 subject to a cap of net debt / EBITDA at 3.5 times throughout the project period.
